# About

## Mission

AIBHS trains the workforce in healthcare and biomedical sciences to develop and implement trustworthy artificial
intelligence technologies. The program focuses on transforming digital patient care, translational research, and
precision medicine.

## Program structure

- **Degree:** Master's degree, experiential learning graduate program
- **Full-time completion:** 1.5 years
- **Format:** Hybrid — online and face-to-face
- **Campuses:** Gainesville and Jacksonville, FL
- **Start date:** Fall 2026
- **Pace:** Flexible for full-time and part-time students

## Key features

- AI design studios
- Clinical design studios
- Hands-on experiential learning electives

## Who it's for

- Traditional graduate students
- Working professionals
- Combined professional/graduate degree students
- Full-time and part-time students

## Organizational structure

AIBHS is offered through the [Intelligent Clinical Care Center (IC3)](https://ic3.center.ufl.edu/education/aibhs/)
at the University of Florida College of Medicine.

## How to apply

Applications go through [ApplyWeb](https://www.applyweb.com/uflgrad/index.ftl).
