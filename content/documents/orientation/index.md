# Orientation

**[Download the PDF ↓](../../data/documents/AIBHS_Orientation.pdf)**

**AIBHS Orientation**

**Orientation Date:** Tuesday, August 18
**Time:** 5:30–7:30 PM

## Locations

- Jacksonville: 801 W Bay Street, Room 231
- Gainesville: Malachowsky Hall, Room 2400

## Purpose

Introduce students to the culture, vision, and collaborative nature of AIBHS through our
Campus Without Borders philosophy. Students from both campuses will participate
together in a shared orientation experience.

## Agenda

- Welcome and AIBHS Vision
- Kahoot Icebreaker
- Faculty Introductions and Research Highlights
- Program Overview (curriculum, expectations, resources, key milestones)
- Innovation Showcase (Digital Twin and Gainesville robot demonstration)
- Networking

## Notes

- Light refreshments will be provided at both campuses.
- A faculty member teaching in Jacksonville will be present on-site.
- Students unable to attend in person may request a virtual participation link in advance.
- Formal program concludes around 7:00 PM, followed by networking until 7:30 PM.

## Goal

Ensure students leave feeling connected as one inaugural AIBHS community, regardless of
campus location, while reinforcing the program's collaborative and innovative culture.
