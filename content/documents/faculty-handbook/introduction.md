# Introduction to UF AIBHS

**[Download the PDF ↓](../../data/documents/AIBHS_Faculty_Handbook.pdf)**

The Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence in Biomedical and Health Sciences
(AIBHS) at the University of Florida is an innovative, interdisciplinary program
designed to prepare the next generation of leaders at the intersection of artificial
intelligence, healthcare, and biomedical research. Through a unique blend of
coursework, experiential learning, and mentorship from both AI and clinical experts,
students gain the knowledge and practical skills needed to develop and implement
trustworthy AI solutions that improve patient care, advance translational research, and
drive innovation in precision medicine. Offered across UF's Gainesville and Jacksonville
campuses with flexible learning options, AIBHS equips graduates to address some of
healthcare's most complex challenges in an increasingly AI-driven world.
