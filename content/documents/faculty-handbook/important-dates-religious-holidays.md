# Religious Holidays

**[Download the PDF ↓](../../data/documents/AIBHS_Faculty_Handbook.pdf)**

Students wishing to observe a religious holiday must provide notice well in advance.
Those students are still responsible for completing any missed material, but should be
provided with a reasonable amount of time to make up the work.

The full policy may be found below:

<https://policy.ufl.edu/regulation/4-052/>
