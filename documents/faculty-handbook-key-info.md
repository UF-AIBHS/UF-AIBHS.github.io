# Key Information — Faculty Handbook

## Program basics

- 1.5-year program, 30 credits to graduate, cohorts start each fall
- Offered on both the Gainesville and Jacksonville campuses

## Onboarding

- Complete New Faculty Orientation and College of Medicine faculty handbook requirements
- Required myTraining modules: HIPAA/Privacy, FERPA, Compliance and Ethics, Mandatory
  Reporting, IRB, Title IX, Supporting Students in Distress

## Canvas

- Request a Canvas shell (registrar or non-registrar) before the semester starts
- Add TAs via Instructor Tools → Manage Users → Add Users (enter UFID, set section and role)
- Other systems tied into Canvas: EdStream (messaging), Gradescope and Kaizen (grading),
  Honorlock (proctoring), Ally (accessibility reports)

## Student accommodations

- Handled through the Disability Resource Center (DRC)
- Extra time and deadline extensions are set per-student in Canvas quiz/assignment settings

## Office hours & communication

- Minimum 2 hours/week of office time per course, not by-appointment-only
- Post office hours somewhere visible (syllabus, Canvas, office door)
- Primary communication channel is UF email; aim to respond within 48 business hours

## Syllabus

- Submit your syllabus to the Program Specialist for Simple Syllabus (UF's required
  syllabus system) before the semester starts

## End of semester

- Final exams follow the university's published schedule — no unapproved moves
- Last 3 days of classes + reading days: no new final/comprehensive exams or take-home
  finals due; previously-announced regular assignments are still fine
- Submit final grades through ONE.UF by the published deadline
- Encourage students to complete GatorEvals

## Important dates & policies

- [Academic calendar](https://gradcatalog.ufl.edu/graduate/calendar/) — add/drop window,
  reading days
- [Religious holiday policy](https://policy.ufl.edu/regulation/4-052/) — students must give
  advance notice
- [Reading days policy](https://policy.ufl.edu/regulation/7-055/) — nothing mandatory may
  be scheduled
